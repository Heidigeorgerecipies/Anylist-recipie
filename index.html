Rosemary Thyme Brown Butter Rice Krispie Treats

Adapted for personal recipe importing from Stough Barlow Team. The timing of the additional marshmallows is interpreted as folding them in at the end.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz (2 sticks) salted butter
  • 4 sprigs fresh thyme, plus extra leaves for garnish
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • 2 (10 oz) bags mini marshmallows
  • 2 cups additional mini marshmallows
  • 4 cups Rice Krispies cereal
  • 4 cups Corn Flakes cereal
  • 1 cup pecan pieces
  • Flaky sea salt, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Line a 9 × 9-inch baking dish with parchment paper.
  2. Melt the salted butter in a large, heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat.
  3. Add thyme and rosemary sprigs. Cook, stirring constantly, until butter foams and the milk solids turn golden brown.
  4. Remove and discard herb sprigs.
  5. Add the two 10-ounce bags of mini marshmallows and stir until melted.
  6. Turn off heat. Stir in Rice Krispies, Corn Flakes and pecans until coated.
  7. Fold in the additional 2 cups of mini marshmallows.
  8. Transfer to prepared pan and gently press into an even layer.
  9. Sprinkle with thyme leaves and flaky sea salt. Cool completely.
  10. Cut into squares and serve.
